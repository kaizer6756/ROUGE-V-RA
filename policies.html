Store Policies

Your transparency and satisfaction matter to us.

Privacy Policy

We collect personal information necessary to complete orders, provide support, and improve our services. This may include your name, email, phone number, shipping address, order history, and account details.

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Information is used only to process orders, handle customer support, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

Shipping Policy

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Standard shipping is ₱100.00 and delivery time usually ranges from 3–5 business days depending on your location. Express delivery options may be offered at checkout when available.

Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns or exchanges for damaged, defective, or incorrect items. Please contact support within the stated return period and include proof of purchase. Products must be unused, sealed, and returned in their original packaging where possible.

Payment Policy

We accept secure payment methods available at checkout. Your selected method will be verified before order confirmation. Payment details are never stored in plain text and are processed through secure systems.
